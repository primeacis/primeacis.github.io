Privacy Policy

App: City Color  |  Developer: Primeacis  |  Last updated: September 28, 2026

City Color is a free map-coloring puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data We Collect

City Color itself collects no personal data. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal information. The Advertising section below explains what our ad provider, Unity Ads, collects to serve and measure ads.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves a small number of preferences locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. These values never leave your device:

  • Your best (fewest) moves and best (fastest) time for each level
  • Your chosen visual theme
  • Your sound effects on/off preference

This data is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

City Color displays interstitial (full-screen) ads provided by Unity Ads, shown when you tap Home after solving a level. To serve and measure ads, Unity Ads may collect and share data from your device, including approximate location, personal and device identifiers (such as your Advertising ID), purchase history, app activity such as page views and taps, and app diagnostics. Data is encrypted in transit, and you can request its deletion through Unity. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings under Google > Ads.

For more information on how Unity handles this data, see the Unity Privacy Policy.

Internet & Network Access

City Color uses the internet to load and display ads via Unity Ads. No personal data is transmitted by City Color itself.

Children

City Color is intended for users 13 and older. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page will be revised.
