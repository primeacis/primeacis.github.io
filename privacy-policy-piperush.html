Privacy Policy

App: Pipe Rush  |  Developer: Primeacis  |  Last updated: September 8, 2026

Pipe Rush is a free pipe-connecting puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal information.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves a small number of preferences locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. These values never leave your device:

  • Your best score (fewest cells used) for each level
  • Your chosen visual theme
  • Your sound effects on/off preference

This data is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

Pipe Rush does not currently display any advertisements. This page will be updated, along with the app's Play Store data safety disclosures, if that changes.

Internet & Network Access

Pipe Rush does not currently require an internet connection to play, and no personal data is transmitted by the app.

Children

Pipe Rush is suitable for all ages. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page will be revised.
