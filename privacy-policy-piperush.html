Privacy Policy

App: Nodeline Rush  |  Developer: Primeacis  |  Last updated: September 25, 2026

Nodeline Rush is a free pipe-connecting puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data We Collect

Nodeline Rush itself collects no personal data. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal information. Our advertising provider, Unity Ads, does collect certain device and usage data to serve ads — see the Advertising section below.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves a small number of preferences locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. These values never leave your device:

  • Your best score (fewest cells used) for each level
  • Your chosen visual theme
  • Your sound effects on/off preference

This data is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

Nodeline Rush displays ads provided by Unity Ads. Unity Ads collects and shares data such as approximate location, personal and device identifiers (including your Advertising ID), IP address, app activity such as page views and taps, and app diagnostics, to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. Data is encrypted in transit, and you can request deletion through Unity. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings under Google > Ads.

For more information on how Unity handles data, see the Unity Privacy Policy.

Internet & Network Access

The app uses the internet to load and display ads via Unity Ads. The game itself can be played offline, and Nodeline Rush does not itself transmit any personal data.

Children

Nodeline Rush is intended for users aged 13 and over. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page will be revised.
