Privacy Policy

App: Hanoi Rush  |  Developer: Primeacis  |  Last updated: August 18, 2026

Hanoi Rush is a free puzzle game based on the classic Tower of Hanoi. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal information.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves a small number of preferences locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. These values never leave your device:

  • Your best move count for each disk count (3–9 disks)
  • Your chosen visual theme

This data is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Multiplayer & Firebase

When you use the multiplayer mode, the app connects to Google Firebase Realtime Database to create and join game rooms. The following temporary data is written to Firebase:

  • A randomly generated anonymous user ID (not linked to your identity)
  • Room code, disk count, and move count during an active game session

This data is used solely to synchronise the game between two players in real time. Room data is not associated with any personal information and may be automatically cleaned up after the session ends. Firebase is operated by Google; see Firebase Privacy and Security for details.

Advertising

Hanoi Rush does not currently display any advertisements.

Internet & Network Access

The app uses the internet only for the multiplayer feature (Firebase). No personal data is transmitted by Hanoi Rush itself.

Children

Hanoi Rush is suitable for all ages. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page will be revised.
