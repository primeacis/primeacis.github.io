Privacy Policy

App: Knight Circuit  |  Developer: Primeacis  |  Last updated: September 24, 2026

Knight Circuit is a free knight's-tour puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data We Collect

Knight Circuit itself collects no personal data. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information, and has no accounts. The advertising provider described below does collect certain device data — see the Advertising section.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves a small number of preferences locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. These values never leave your device:

  • Your best move count and fastest time for each board size and tour type
  • Your Daily Puzzle streak
  • Your chosen visual theme
  • Your sound effects, hint overlay, and default start-square preferences

This data is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

Knight Circuit displays interstitial (full-screen) ads provided by Unity Ads, shown when you leave the results screen after finishing a puzzle. To serve and measure ads, Unity Ads may collect and share data from your device, such as your device's Advertising ID and other device identifiers, approximate location, and information about how you interact with ads and the app. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

For more information on how Unity handles this data, see Unity's Privacy Policy.

Internet & Network Access

Knight Circuit uses the internet solely to load and display ads through Unity Ads. The game itself can be played without a connection. No personal data is transmitted by Knight Circuit itself.

Children

Knight Circuit is intended for users 13 and older. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page will be revised.
