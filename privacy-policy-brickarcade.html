Privacy Policy

App: Brick Arcade  |  Developer: Sanket Mathur  |  Last updated: August 18, 2026

Brick Arcade is a free arcade game. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal information.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves a small number of preferences locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. These values never leave your device:

  • High scores for each game (Blocks, Race, Dot)
  • Your chosen starting level
  • Sound on/off preference

This data is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

Brick Arcade displays rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID to serve relevant ads. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings under Google > Ads.

For more information on how Google uses data from apps that use AdMob, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Internet & Network Access

The app uses the internet solely to load and display ads via Google AdMob. No personal data is transmitted by Brick Arcade itself.

Children

Brick Arcade is suitable for all ages. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page will be revised.
